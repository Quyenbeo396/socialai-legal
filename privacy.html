Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

SocialAI Agent is a desktop application that helps users prepare and manage social media content, including uploading selected videos to TikTok drafts where supported.

2. Information and Permissions

When users connect a TikTok account, the application may receive basic profile information and access permissions authorized through TikTok Login Kit. The application may use the video upload permission to upload videos selected by the user to TikTok drafts.

3. How Information Is Used

Information and permissions are used to support account connection and the application's requested features. The application should only access information needed for these functions and within the permissions granted by the user.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be processed or stored locally or by services used by the application, depending on the application's implementation and configuration. Users should protect their devices and account credentials. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect authentication tokens and other sensitive information.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

The application interacts with TikTok when users authorize the integration and use supported features. Information handled by TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own privacy policy and terms. The application maintainer does not intend to sell users' personal information.

6. User Choices

Users can choose whether to connect their TikTok account and which videos to upload. Users may revoke the application's access through the relevant account or platform settings. Revoking access may prevent connected features from working.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Retention and deletion depend on where information is stored and how the application is configured. Users should remove local application data they no longer need and revoke account access when appropriate.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.
